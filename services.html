Services

Anything that can be made digital

Ten things I build the most, listed properly below. If your job doesn't fit neatly into one of these, get in touch anyway. That's usually the interesting part.

How a build actually happens

Websites

Custom builds from scratch, coded to fit your business, not a page builder template wearing your logo. Fast, mobile first, and easy for you to keep updated.

Apps

Web and mobile ready apps built to actually get used, not just demoed once and forgotten. Loyalty, bookings, galleries, whatever your customers need in their pocket.

POS systems

Point of sale that fits how your business actually runs. Till, stock and staff, working together instead of three separate systems fighting each other.

E-commerce

Stores built to sell. Checkout, stock levels and delivery sorted properly, so you're not chasing orders in a spreadsheet at midnight.

Booking & scheduling

Systems that stop the back and forth messages for good. Staff calendars, deposits, automatic reminders, and fewer no-shows.

QR ordering

QR menus, loyalty cards and check ins. Scan and go, nothing for your customer to install and nothing extra for your staff to manage.

Automations

The repetitive stuff in your business, handled without you touching it. Emails, stock alerts, follow ups, sorted quietly in the background.

Integrations

Getting the tools you already use to actually talk to each other, so your bookings, payments and stock stop living in separate corners of your business.

AI search

Search that understands what people mean, not just what they typed. Handy for a big catalogue, a knowledge base, or customers who don't know the exact product name.

Custom software

Anything else that runs your business. Dashboards, internal tools, member systems. If it doesn't exist yet off the shelf, that's usually where I'm most useful.
